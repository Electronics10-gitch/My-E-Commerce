# II. Statement of the Problem

Buyers looking specifically for electronics currently have to work across several general-purpose marketplaces (e.g., Konga, Jumia, AliExpress, Slot, Jiji) to compare prices, authenticity and seller reliability, with no single platform dedicated to electronics that consolidates this comparison for them. Sellers of electronics, in turn, lack a focused channel where their listings are not diluted among unrelated product categories, and both sides face recurring friction around trust (fake or mismatched listings), fragmented communication, and inconsistent post-purchase support (tracking, returns and feedback).

There is therefore a need for a dedicated, cross-platform electronics marketplace that lets sellers list and manage products, lets buyers search, compare and purchase with confidence, and lets both interact securely — through authentication, a reliable cart/checkout/order flow, real-time chat support, and transparent price comparison against the platforms buyers already use — while remaining compliant with applicable data-protection and consumer-protection requirements. ElectroMart is proposed as the solution to this problem.
